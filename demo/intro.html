Walrus Proof Agent

Problem

Agent decisions are hard to audit.

AI agents can review grants, trigger trades, and run operations, but their evidence and reasoning often disappear into app logs.

Solution

Walrus stores evidence. Sui mints the receipt.

Walrus Proof Agent stores the full proof payload on Walrus and mints a compact Sui ProofReceipt that reviewers can verify.
